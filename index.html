The Chongqing Cultural, Sports, Education and Tourism Association of Australia (CSETA) is a Melbourne-based nonprofit building real exchange between Chongqing and Australia: specific people, specific places, specific projects.

近期动态

What we've been doing

六月，协会在墨尔本为重庆直辖二十九周年办了一场城市音乐电影首映；九月，川渝文化展位走进联邦广场。这些相遇正在重庆和墨尔本两边继续发生。

In June we marked Chongqing's 29th anniversary with a city music film premiere in Melbourne. In September we brought Sichuan–Chongqing culture to Federation Square. The work continues in both cities.

2026.09.19Federation Square

川渝文化亮相墨尔本联邦广场

Sichuan–Chongqing culture at Federation Square

2026 中国传统文化节在联邦广场举行。协会与澳洲川渝文化教育体育协会、HYSTON 团队共同呈现川渝文化主题展位：熊猫意象、巴渝服饰、重庆城市影像、青年创意内容，以及《重庆市民》Live Film 与重庆直辖二十九周年活动回顾。不少本地观众在展位前停下来，问起去重庆旅行、学习和合作的可能。

At the Chinese Traditional Cultural Festival 2026 in Federation Square, CSETA, the Sichuan-Chongqing Culture, Education and Sports Association of Australia and HYSTON presented a Sichuan–Chongqing booth: panda imagery, Bayu-inspired clothing, Chongqing city film, youth creative work, and a look back at the Chongqing Citizen premiere. Many visitors stopped to ask about travel, study and partnerships in Chongqing.

到访展位及出席开幕的嘉宾包括：维州多元文化事务部长 Paul Edbrooke MP、中国驻墨尔本副总领事刘东源、联邦众议员 Gabriel Ng MP、墨尔本市议员 Gladys Liu 与 Rafael Camillo、维州议员 John Mullahy MP。

Guests at the opening and the booth included the Hon Paul Edbrooke MP, Victorian Minister for Multicultural Affairs; Deputy Consul-General Liu Dongyuan; Gabriel Ng MP; Councillors Gladys Liu and Rafael Camillo; and John Mullahy MP.

“文化真正的意义，不只是把一个地方展示出来，而是让原本不认识的人，因为它走到一起。”— 会长 Hyston Huang
“The meaning of culture is not simply to display where we come from. It is to bring together people who may never otherwise have met.”— Hyston Huang, President

《重庆市民》城市音乐 Live Film 首映暨重庆直辖二十九周年活动

Chongqing Citizen: City Music Live Film premiere and Chongqing's 29th anniversary

由协会主办、HYSTON 团队制作，维多利亚州政府支持。作品由三十首原创歌曲与三十个电影章节构成，以坡坎街道、长江水岸、轻轨穿楼、洪崖洞夜景为背景，分上篇《山城来信》与下篇《江水回声》两幕放映。

Hosted by CSETA, produced by HYSTON and supported by the Victorian Government. Thirty original songs and thirty film chapters set among Chongqing's stairways, riverbanks and hillside light rail, screened in two parts: A Letter from the Mountain City and Echoes of the River.

Return visit to Chongqing Art Market

「山城力量计划」正式发布

Mountain City Power Initiative launched

由白马市市长 Cr Kirsten Langford 与 Hyston 共同发起。首阶段聚焦重庆山区学校与女性教育工作者，通过品牌直寄、物资认捐和社区资源连接，送去女性用品、教学物资与生活支持。

Co-initiated by Whitehorse Mayor Cr Kirsten Langford and Hyston. Phase one supports mountain-area schools and women educators in Chongqing through direct brand shipments, pledged supplies and community connections.

About · 关于协会

交流不是一次性的合影

Exchange is more than a photo op

从山城到海岸，从火锅到球场，从课堂到旅途。协会是在澳大利亚维多利亚州注册的非营利组织，HYSTON 提供创意与数字支持。

From mountain city to coastline, hotpot to tennis court, classroom to journey. CSETA is a nonprofit incorporated in Victoria, Australia, with creative and digital support from HYSTON.
